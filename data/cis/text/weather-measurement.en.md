---
title: "How strong is weather measurement?"
---

We use two metrics to assess the ability to measure weather and climate accurately on the ground and from space. The first is the weather station density index, which is a composite index based on the number of weather stations, the coverage area of the weather stations, the administrative level area, and the coverage rate per administrative level. Secondly, we used the optical satellite data availability index, which corresponds to the inverted annual mean cloud cover fraction. Higher values in both metrics indicate stronger weather measurements on the ground and from space, respectively.
