DETREND / ANOMALY POLICY (caveat f — argued BETTER than faostat single-OLS):
anomaly_pct = 100 × (y − era_median) / era_median, computed per county×crop×season×ERA, qc_flag=0 rows only.
Why not the faostat linear-detrend: (1) a single OLS across both eras lets the 2013 district→county remap level-shift masquerade as trend and biases era residuals in opposite signs — that leaks provenance artefacts straight into every driver composite; (2) per-era OLS over 9–11 points confounds trend with ENSO decadal clustering — era B ends with the 2020–22 triple-dip La Niña and 2023 El Niño, so a fitted era-B slope would absorb the very droughts we want to show and shrink their anomalies. Era-MEDIAN centering removes both trend (negligible within ≤11-yr windows) and the level shift, is robust to the handful of outliers, and reads to policymakers as "% of a usual year". Gate: an era needs ≥7 qc-clean years to earn a median; below that its points render absolute-only (greyed in anomaly views, note printed). Annex A5 carries a single-OLS-detrend sensitivity toggle; the story layer never switches.
Annual view: NO anomaly analytics at all — annual calendar totals mix both rain seasons, so season attribution is mushy; Annual is context-only (levels + optional faint OLS trend line), which also sidesteps its own remap-boundary detrend problem.

qc_flag POLICY (caveat d): mark-and-exclude-from-statistics. Flag semantics are upstream (HarvestStat v1.2, Lee et al. 2025) and not re-derived here, so we neither trust nor silently delete: flagged points stay visible as hollow/hatched glyphs with "QC-flagged upstream (HarvestStat)" in the tooltip, and are excluded from era medians, bin medians and counts. Cost is 7 seasonal rows — negligible; captions report both counts ("n=20 used, 0 flagged excluded").

- PROVENANCE (caveat c): thin top-margin brackets labeling the two segments — "1991–2001 · district-era records remapped to today's counties" / "2015–2024 · county records" — the same segments the anomaly medians use, so the statistics are explainable from the picture.

--- TRADEOFFS ---
REJECTED, with reasons:
1. Single linear detrend across 1991–2024 (the faostat_detrended reuse invited by caveat f): the 2013 district→county remap level-shift would be absorbed as spurious trend and bias the two eras' residuals in opposite signs, contaminating every wet/dry composite. Also rejected per-era OLS slopes: over 9–11 points, "trend" and ENSO decadal clustering are confounded — era B's fitted slope would eat the 2020–22 triple-dip drought signal itself. Chose era-median % anomalies; single-OLS kept only as an annex sensitivity.
2. Lines/area encodings for the season series: any path mark begs the eye to interpolate across 2002–2014, violating caveat (a). Bars + a hatched, labeled hole band; nothing drawn through it.
3. Harvest-year x-axis (the natural column) — would need offset strip lanes and per-mark lag annotations. Planting-year indexing makes the lag grammar (caveat e) correct by construction: the Short bar sits over its OND cell. Cost: "2023 Short" means harvest Mar-2024, which the tooltip and table make explicit.
4. Boxplots / CIs / r-values in the story-layer composites: n=5–8 per tercile bin per season — quartiles and correlations are noise at that n. Dots + median tick + honest counts ("k of n"); inference (OLS fit, r/n/p) routed to annex A5 (caveat g).
5. NAPR/HarvestStat as adjacent A/B panels sharing a section (V2-52's literal wording): adjacency plus a warning caption is weaker than structural separation — moved NAPR levels to §1 stakes and HarvestStat to the new §3, cross-referenced by the "Two rulers" callout. Flagged as a deliberate deviation for Pete to ratify; fallback = A/B sub-sections in §3.2, never shared axes.
6. Driver-colored strips inside the new §3: would re-couple the dry/wet-impact section to ENSO/IOD and defeat V2-51's purpose; §3 uses tercile-colored strips (season outcome), driver color stays §2. The single bridge kept is the optional "driver phase" anchor in 3.8-B's composite view.
7. Sorghum in seasonal views (2 harvest years — no series) and hard-dropping qc_flag>0 rows (hides real data) or silently including them in medians (imports upstream-suspect values into statistics): mark-and-exclude-from-stats instead, with both counts in every caption.
8. Any county-level yield×driver regression/beta map or gap in-filling (interpolation, national scaling): the two-era, ~20-season record legitimately supports composites-by-category and counts, not fitted surfaces — and caveat (a) forbids in-fill outright.
9. Live forecast joins from third parties in the §4 chips: house rule is Kenya-Met/CPC-state only; the chips therefore show historical tercile composites keyed to KMD forecast language, not model predictions.

The harvest date is carried by tooltip ("Short rains 2023: planted Oct 2023, harvested ~Mar 2024 — 12,400 t") and by the visible caption ("Short-rains bars sit on the OND season that grew them"). The x-axis label reads "growing season (year the rains fell)".
